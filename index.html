UpStream: adaptive live video super-resolution

A live streaming system that decides, frame by frame, whether to send 720p directly or send lower resolution and upscale ×2 or ×4 on the GPU, within a 33 ms deadline.

  • Designed UpStreamVSR, a 217K-parameter super-resolution model conditioned on the encoder's CRF, with early exits so it can stop when time runs out.
  • Trained on real compressed video (StreamSR) on Kaggle GPUs.
  • Beat CDA-VSR, NanoVSR, SPAN and EfRLFN at every bitrate tested.
  • In the live system: +0.95–1.33 dB viewer PSNR over the same pipeline with published models, at 55–62% of their GPU power. Beat every fixed delivery mode by up to 1.5 dB over tc-netem-shaped TCP.
  • Ablations drove the design: dropping recurrent memory cost under 0.05 dB and made ×4 65% faster; a backlog guard halved late frames and p95 latency.

CropAdapt: plant disease detection from lab to field

Classifiers trained on clean lab photos (PlantVillage) fail on messy field photos (PlantDoc). CropAdapt closes part of that gap across 16 disease classes. It grew out of problems seen on the estate where I work.

  • Fine-tuned DINOv2 ViT-S/14 with attention LoRA, training 0.7% of the weights.
  • Designed a discrimination-guided, feature-weighted MMD loss: it aligns features that shift between domains while leaving the class-separating ones alone, tuned each epoch by a class-separation controller.
  • Ran ablations against standard MMD, bounded weights and controller smoothing.
